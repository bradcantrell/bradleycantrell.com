The Landscape Matrix

The ten weekly exercises work cumulatively to explore the complexities of landscape at multiple scales and time periods through both abstract and concrete representational techniques, 2D and 3D projection, and static and dynamic media. Each assignment is located within a landscape matrix — a range of landscape typologies that must be mediated in an attempt to represent hybrids and transitions between each region.
