No standalone Project 1 brief survives in the archive; this page is transcribed from the course description and syllabus, which name the assignment both “Sensing and Device Research” and “Device Space Research.”

Research a responsive system (aerial drone, smartphone, etc.) and create a series of diagrams dissecting its sensing technologies. Create a representation of the device or network’s world/vision.

The exercise follows the seminar’s introduction to the Arduino microcontroller and the Firefly plugin. Students were tasked with becoming experts in specific features of the software/hardware and sharing their knowledge through documentation and/or tutorials. Workshops taught by Frank Melendez in the School of Architecture were available to students unfamiliar with Grasshopper.

Objectives

  1. Dissect the sensing technologies of a responsive system through a series of diagrams.
  2. Represent the device or network’s world/vision — how it perceives, senses, and maps its environment.
  3. Document specific software/hardware features and share that knowledge through documentation or tutorials.
