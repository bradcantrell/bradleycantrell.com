Exercise I · Deconstructing Infrastructure

research · representation · precedent
Teams of threeReview: Feb 5–6Timeline · Model · Section · Mapping

Download the full Exercise I brief (PDF)

In the first exercise you are asked to research and represent an infrastructure in the Central Valley of California (in Spring 2019, the Mississippi River Watershed was also an option — see the list of infrastructures). Use this opportunity to situate the infrastructure within a larger historical timeline: when was it constructed? How was it funded? Is it part of a larger infrastructural project? Look closely at the operation of the infrastructure and make direct connections to its context and how it influences the territory in which it is situated. You will work in teams of three.

Deliverables

There are four deliverables for this exercise:

  1. Implementation and Operations Timeline — create a timeline that layers the construction and implementation of the infrastructure, the ongoing operation, maintenance, and any significant events occurring throughout the infrastructure’s life. 11″×17″ landscape orientation, print and PDF.

  2. Rhino Model — representing the overall form of the infrastructure and important elements of the context. Single view (white materials for infrastructure, grey for context), 11″×17″ landscape orientation with annotated overlay (dimensions, operations, etc), print and PDF, 3dm file.

  3. Illustrative Section Cut of Infrastructure — highlighting the relationship with the context. Scale ~1:10 or 1:20, 24″×36″ landscape orientation (minimal use of color, rely on linework), print and PDF.

  4. Mapping of Infrastructure and Context — focus on the operation of the infrastructure (from the timeline) and how it alters, modifies, and choreographs the landscape. Attempt to not only document current operations but to also think projectively about the potentials of alternate forms of operations, methods of resistance, and direct hacks. Scale TBD, 24″×36″ landscape orientation (minimal use of color, rely on linework), print and PDF.
