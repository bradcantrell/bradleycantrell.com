Exercise II · Simulating Dynamic Phenomena

simulation · animation · speculation
Teams of 2–3Workshops: CFD · RealFlowSimulation · Animation

Download the full Exercise II brief (PDF)

In the second exercise you will work in teams of 2–3, experimenting with simulation software, to create animations and drawings of dynamic phenomena and infrastructure. Using the models of infrastructure created in Exercise 01, create simulations of fluid (water/air) flows that test the infrastructure’s relationship to topography, massing, and/or possible reconfigurations/modifications. Each team will illustrate current conditions of the infrastructure and then create a range of alternative configurations that propose speculative futures for the infrastructure.

Considerations

Consider the following when developing the deliverables for this exercise:

  • How can the geometry and processes be abstracted/simplified to increase the efficacy of the simulations?
  • How does the simulation highlight/test a specific aspect of the current infrastructure?
  • Using the simulation tools, how might the team propose alternative configurations?

When developing the alternative configurations be imaginative, use the simulation and resultant visualizations to highlight the phenomena rather than the infrastructure and/or landscape. Software will be introduced in workshops, but it is encouraged to also explore alternative software and/or techniques (physical modeling).

If the team prefers to use another subject rather than the infrastructure from Exercise 01 that is possible — for example, a team’s studio project may be used to develop the deliverables for this exercise.

Deliverables

  1. Document, 11″ × 5.5″ portrait orientation, PDF format, including:

    • Short narrative describing the current infrastructure and the simulation tests
    • Images, annotated, that show the current infrastructure and simulation tests
    • Short narrative describing alternative configurations
    • Images, annotated, that show the alternate/speculative infrastructure and simulation tests

  2. Animation(s) showing multiple configurations and/or current conditions.
