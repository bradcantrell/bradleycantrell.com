Exercise III · Sensing the Environment

sensing · monitoring · actuation
Teams of threeWorkshops: Arduino · FireflyPrecedent · Speculation

Download the full Exercise III brief (PDF)

In the third exercise you will work in teams of three (3) to develop a prototype responsive device situated within the historical context of surveying and monitoring. The exercise is broken into two discrete components: the first is the development of a sensing and monitoring precedent that documents the technologies and deployment of current devices and methods. When researching the precedent, keep in mind the temporal scale, the physical context/scale, typical deployments, the scope, and the data gathered. Be rigorous and precise with the documentation as well as creative and generative. Use your representational tools to create illustrations of these technologies that are landscape specific and design oriented.

The second is the design and proposal of a responsive device/installation that incorporates sensing, monitoring, and/or actuation. The device is a synthesis of three methods of inquiry — the sensing and monitoring precedents, further research to uncover creative uses of responsive technologies, and experimentation with physical computing. Using current sites/research from your design studio or other sources, develop a responsive installation that may be used to record, visualize, and/or choreograph landscape phenomena. Ground the concept within the three modes of inquiry but be experimental with the proposal.

Deliverables

Precedent

  1. Surveying and Monitoring Precedent — create a document, 11″ × 5.5″ portrait orientation, PDF format, including:

    • Short narrative describing the technology and use cases
    • Timeline of the surveying or monitoring precedent, documenting the history, use, and associated technologies
    • Diagrams and/or illustrations (from the 3D model, below) showing spatial relationships, deployment, etc.

  2. Rhino Model — representing the overall form/proportions of the precedent, 3dm file.

What’s Cool?

  1. Collect 3–5 innovative uses of sensing and monitoring technologies and/or tools currently being used in landscape architecture, environmental design, environmental science, agriculture, etc. Present these with notes explaining the use of the technology and why it is important for your speculation.

Speculation

  1. Proposition showing how sensing and monitoring technology may be deployed in an innovative, creative, generative intervention:

    • Appropriate design representation to express the concept, implementation, and materiality
    • Precedents that situate the concept

  2. Physical or virtual prototype that serves as a technological proof of concept. Using physical computing, create a working prototype that highlights the sensing, monitoring, and/or actuation proposed.
