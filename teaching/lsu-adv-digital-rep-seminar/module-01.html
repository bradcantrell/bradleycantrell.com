Assignment

You have developed models of specific blocks in downtown Baton Rouge and it is now time to represent the space using animation and still imagery. You are to create a series of rendered stills and a single animation in order to convey the essence of your space. You are welcome to use any methods you choose to develop your images and animation. You may incorporate your fellow students models in this work.
