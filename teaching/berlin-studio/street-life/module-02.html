002 · Media

media and analysis · representing the street
Launch: Sep 14Review: Sep 30Deliverables due Oct 7

Download the full 002 brief (PDF)

In the second phase of research you will work together to represent the streetscape of Berlin focusing on 3 specific corridors. The corridors are situated in three neighborhoods that are facing a diverse range of issues in regards to gentrification.

  1. Schöneberg — Nollendorfplatz to Hauptstraße via Maaßstraße – Goltzstraße – Akazienstraße
  2. Kreuzberg / Friedrichshain — Wrangelstraße from Mariannenplatz to Taborstraße
  3. Friedrichshain — Eldenaer Straße to Revaler Straße via Simon Dach Straße – Niederbarnimstraße – Proskauer Straße

GIS Base · CAD Base · Sections · Massing Model

To complete the work in this stage of the research you will work as a full studio, in groups of four, and as individuals.

GIS Base

Working together as a full studio you will create a GIS Base that contains building footprints, roadways, and waterways at a minimum. This GIS base will cover the Greater Berlin metropolitan area. Using this base you will export a .dwg file that will be used by each group to reference their CAD base and massing models.

CAD Base

Working together in groups of 4 you will create the CAD base, georeferenced to the previous DWG that was exported from GIS. Each group will focus on the corridor assigned as the highest level of detail and will then determine the bounds of their base map. There may be multiple levels of detail moving away from the corridor itself. As a group you will choose which features to add to your CAD base — street markings, light poles, signage, vegetation, paving patterns, overhangs, etc. Think critically about what is important and develop a strategy to distribute and complete the CAD drawing.

Sections

Each group will complete a minimum of 4 sections that cut perpendicular across the assigned corridor. The section locations should represent specific moments of importance, highlighting a corridor's character, a specific node, or a unique characteristic. The sections will augment the CAD base and illustrate in greater detail concepts such as material assemblies, soil/paving, and spatial relationships.

Massing Model

Using the CAD base and/or GIS, each group will create a massing model that will represent the corridor and surrounding urban form. The massing model should use minimal color and focus on urban form, which goes beyond just building footprints and voids. Determine as a team the extents of the massing model. Then determine what features need to be the focus of the model, i.e. topography, building mass/stories, vegetation, etc.

Individual Media Experiments

As you are working with your team to develop the base representations of your corridor you should also begin developing concepts for the creation of a representational experiment. Use this as a way to explore a topic that interests you; it may come from the previous research assignment or may be a completely new exploration. This experimentation should situate itself in one or more of the following concepts.

  • Temporal — An exploration of time and how its effects play out in the streetscape, i.e. program/use, construction/maintenance, history, etc.
  • Aesthetic — How might aesthetics play a role, where do they come from and how might a specific style of drawing be used to uncover or illustrate specific ideas.
  • Craft — Using a specific technique and the careful construction of the representation itself, how does the craft of creating the media form a heuristic that helps to further understand the site/streetscape.

The product should explore a view of the streetscape, i.e. plan, aerial, perspectival, section, etc. It can be drawings, realtime gaming environment, stop-motion animation, digital models, physical models, simulations, etc. Use the base materials created (massing, GIS, CAD, sections) as the basis for your representation. Using measured drawings as the basis for the work will ground it physically, allowing your experiment to develop a tension between the technical and ephemeral aspects of the site. What is critical at this stage is that you are experimenting with media to ask questions about the corridor and to use the media as a way to develop a conversation between the physical landscape/streetscape and media.
