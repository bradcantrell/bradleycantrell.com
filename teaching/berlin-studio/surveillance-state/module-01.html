ALAR 8010 — Phase 01 Brief

Method

Phase 01 seeks to unravel the complexities of surveillance in contemporary urbanism. The objectives of this phase of work are:

  1. Become familiar with the broad range of technologies, protocols, perceptions, and actors that have formed around sensing, monitoring, and acquisition of data within the built environment.
  2. Establish relationships between the aforementioned elements to spatialize the implications of surveillance in the city of Berlin.

Working in small teams (2–3) you will develop a deep understanding of one of the following topics:

Research Topics

1. Topological and typological surveillance forms

Irmak Fermen, Huining Zhou

  • Panopticon
  • Topography
  • Fortification
  • Monument
  • Infrastructure — spatial layout / multiple scales; aesthetic

2. State actors and the use of surveillance

Reid Farnsworth, Shaoyu Chen, Jingjing Ji

  • China, USA, Germany, India
  • Who is being surveilled? — states; citizens
  • Cultural Perceptions — propaganda / messaging; self surveillance

3. Data acquisition and Processing (Big Data)

James Fay, Ben Small

  • Voluntary / Involuntary
  • Local / Remote
  • Rules of Transactions
  • Legal Frameworks

4. Counters

Ian MacPherson, Jiashu Lyu

  • Individuals (whistleblowers, etc.)
  • Organizations
  • Subversion — technological; physical forms

5. Technologies (software, hardware)

Shaoyu Chen, Jingjing Ji

  • Historical context of surveillance technologies — sensing; monitoring; surveying
  • Contemporary surveillance systems — sensing; monitoring; surveying
  • Unintended Consequences, feedback loop

6. Environmental

Adam Johnson, Ellie Riley

  • Subjects — non-human species; biological; climate; hydrologic / soil
  • Motives

7. Corporate actors

Somrita Bandyopadhyay, Alex Wright

  • Google, Facebook, Microsoft, Apple
  • TOS / EULA
  • Exchange, convenience vs privacy

Meta Topics

In this body of research it is imperative that the research aims to outline a broad subset of actors in an attempt to highlight how the following meta topics are pervasive throughout all other topics:

  1. Consent
  2. Uneven application across class / race / gender (control / lack of)
  3. Cultural Perception
  4. Aesthetics

The research aims to highlight the range of ways that surveillance unevenly affects communities based on class and race. It should produce a comprehensive body of work for the entire studio and be framed as such — be conscious of your biases to produce a body of work that allows for future studio phases to operationalize the research in multiple ways.

Deliverables

  1. Abstract / Narrative, ~1500 words
  2. Diagrams, Timelines, and mappings, specific to each research topic
  3. PDF Publication, Crown Quarto format, 184mm × 242mm, based on template
  4. Webpage(s) on the studio website, including links, videos, resources, etc.
  5. Zotero Bibliography
