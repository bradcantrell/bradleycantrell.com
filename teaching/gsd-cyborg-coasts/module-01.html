The first phase involves site research and the construction of an onto-cartography that maps site phenomena, processes, and interactions. This onto-cartography will function as the selected reality in which your prototype will evolve. During this first phase students will also experiment with digital and analog models as well as basic prototypes that highlight the proof of a concept. The work should be approached as a form of design research used to produce a rigorous exploration of the implementation of responsive technologies and adaptive systems.

Site — Southern Louisiana

The work will be accomplished in groups of 2–3 students and will focus on sites within Southern Louisiana that have been identified in the 2012 Louisiana Coastal Master Plan. There is latitude in the selection of a site and may be expanded from the master plan if necessary. Based on the range of interventions proposed in the 2012 Master Plan the site research, onto-cartography, and prototypes should outline a proposal for a new coastal infrastructure. This infrastructure will fit within the categories of projects outlined in the master plan or make the case for a new categorization. It is important that through this work a clear trajectory is developed for the second phase of semester.

The Onto-Cartography

The choice of media and technique is an open question for each of the groups when developing the onto-cartography. The onto-cartography should intelligently represent existing and proposed relationships between objects (machines) within the site.

  • What is the relationship between the onto-cartographic representation and the physical site?
  • How can this representation promote iterative prototyping for responsive technologies?
  • How does the onto-cartography perform as a virtualized process?
  • In what way does the representation provide tangible access to a site process or processes and allow the user to observe, manipulate, or interact with this process?

Deliverables

The Phase 01 deliverables will be presented on March 11th. Typical deliverables for the research and onto-cartography will be video/animation, mappings, and/or documents. The initial prototypes may be digital or physical models and/or simulations.
