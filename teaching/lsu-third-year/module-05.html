assimilate — absorb and integrate.
strategize — devise a strategy or strategies.

The next phase of the project will require you to work with a team of your colleagues to develop a strategy for the implementation of overlapping, evolving program at the Moss Point, MS waterfront.

Assimilate

In order to develop an engaging landscape it will be necessary to develop a thoughtful program that responds to multiple events, users, and environmental conditions that may be either complimentary or in opposition.

The groups will explore the following programmatic themes: industry, entertainment, festival, residence, activity, and retail.

Strategize

Each group will implement their developed site strategy that addresses the following questions:

  • How does the waterfront interface with the current city? How will this relationship evolve over time?
  • How will the site adapt temporally to changing program?
  • How does temporal program express or constrain site phenomena?

Deliverables

You will represent your strategy with diagrams, models, 1:10 site plan, 3–5 section/elevations, and 3–5 perspective renderings.
