Travel Journal

field documentation · New York · Boston
Assigned: Aug 24Pin-up: Sep 3

Download the full Project 01 brief (PDF)

Learning through travel is as much about the path as it is about the destination.

During our trip to New York and Boston we will see many compelling works of landscape architecture. We will also spend as much, if not more time passing through the turbulence and grit of two of the world’s most storied cities. The keeping of a sketchbook is a great tool to help you truly see and understand the places that we will experience.

Documentation

During your trip we are asking that you document through drawings, collages, writing, photo montages, and/or video the places that you go. This documentation is not limited to the specific sites that we see as a class but should include important things that you saw or did outside of the group. Inspiration can come as easily from a famous garden as it can from the arrangement of newspaper stands on a street corner.

Sketching is active

Most importantly, sketching is active. It is your re-presentation of the things that resonate with you. Sometimes just a few lines are enough to be able to recall the essence of a great place later. Often, there just isn’t enough time to make a complete and detailed drawing and a few strokes or notes will just have to do. Other times, you may choose to measure an entire site and draw a plan to truly understand how a particular place works.

Deliverables

The specific types of drawings or tools you use to document the trip is up to you. We will expect that you make a consistent effort to document the elements that are valuable to you on the trip and be able to explain the value of your drawings to the class when we return. A slide show of snapshots is not enough! We will each present our sketchbooks and any additional digital material. You will have a short amount of time to present, so instead of retelling the overall story of the trip, try to focus on specific observations that you made and how you chose to represent them.
