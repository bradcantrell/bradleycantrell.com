Animate

final design proposal
Final Review: Nov 18Site: Moss Point, MS

Download the full Project 05 brief (PDF)

So how will this semester’s work culminate in a product for Moss Point? We have looked at precedents, dissected natural phenomena, analyzed the effects of change on the land, and imagined new programs and uses…

For the remaining 2.5 weeks of the studio we will each make a design proposal based on the programmatic study that you have been working on. The framework plan provides a set of guiding principles and rules that will guide your designs. Your task now is to animate these ideas — to bring them to life — so that the people of Moss Point can partake in and carry on your visions.

Within a very short amount of time you will flesh out the ideas of the framework plans. One of the keys to design is to visualize things at different scales — from the human dimension to the site.

Narrative

In order to quickly move from a framework plan to a design plan you will create a narrative for your design concept. This design narrative could come in the form of words, images, or a combination of the two. In essence, you want to tell a compelling story to your audience.

Visualization

The scale of this project demands a depth of understanding from the large dimensions of the city and region to the size, mobility, and sense of place of the individual person. You will need to work reflexively across these scales, allowing one to inform the other.

Agreements

Finally, before the final review we will return to the framework plans that you came up with as a group and make any changes that your design projects have prompted. In a way, the design is a way to test and push further the agreements that you made with your team on the framework plan.
