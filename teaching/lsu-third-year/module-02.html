Project 02 · 10%

Design Precedents

precedent study · three themed models
Assigned: Sep 8Pin-up: Sep 10Review: Sep 15

Download the full Project 02 brief (PDF)

Understanding a design project and the designer’s intent is an integral component of being a landscape architect. Beyond reading project descriptions, it is important that you develop methods of critical analysis in order to actively engage in design research.

Each student will research a park precedent from the list below. The list represents a large range of parks in many different locations, therefore it requires you to extract the information that is applicable to this project. In order for you to extract this information it will require you to comprehensively research your precedent.

Research

Your research should include but not be limited to:

  • Location, Region, Significance
  • Designers and Constituency
  • History
  • Features
  • Site Plan
  • Site Photos/Images
  • Additional information specific to park, site, region

This information will be created at 11x17 landscape orientation. If created digitally the layout should be at 200 pixels per inch. Cite all sources.

Three Models

From your research you will create three models that explore your design precedent through the lens of the following themes: infrastructure, hydrology and/or ecology, and occupation. Each theme leaves room for interpretation; your interpretation of each should exhibit an understanding of the precedent and will attempt to find similarity or contrasts between themes.

Each model will have a three dimensional spatial quality and should be scaled. The three models will be able to be viewed and understood as a triptych and as single elements. The materiality of each model is important and should be considered representative of site features or dynamics.
