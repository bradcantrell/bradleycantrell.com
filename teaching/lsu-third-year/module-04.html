intervention — come between so as to alter a result or course of events.

Intervene

You have studied seemingly benign phenomena to understand the site at Moss Point, MS through a tightly focused lens. Using a phenomena study developed by one of your classmates, you will develop an intervention that directly manipulates phenomena and/or the perception of the phenomena.

Your proposal should use one of the following devices in order to create your intervention:

  • boardwalks and/or island bridges
  • land expansion or subtraction
  • water/land edge and/or access
