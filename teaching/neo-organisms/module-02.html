Studio Brief — Phase 02

The second phase of the term will focus on the implementation of the micro-cosm, wetware, and the neo-organisms. Each project will create a proposal that will frame how the micro-cosm is used as a generative/analytical tool and use this tool to speculate on how wetware (code/protocols) will form a logic for your neo-organisms (infrastructures).

Micro-Cosm

The micro-cosm will be developed and implemented during this phase of the term. It is important that this process is transparent and documented to provide outsiders a way to comprehend your methodology. What are the variables? What are the results, how are they generated and what are the implications? Are the tools you are developing specific to your process? Are they transferable?

Neo-Organisms

Using the micro-cosm, each project will test a series of neo-organisms that are actuated / controlled / choreographed by wetware. These tests will be documented by an operational taxonomy that will illustrate how the neo-organism produces a series of actions within the environment.

Deliverables

The Phase 02 deliverables will be presented on March 22nd.

  • Refined Onto-Cartography
  • Functioning Micro-cosm
  • Documentation of Micro-cosm process and implementation
  • Neo-organism proposal
  • Site scale operations — plans / sections / diagrams / animations
  • Perspectival illustrations — views / animations
  • Operational Taxonomy — catalog, spatialized / mapping
  • Prototypes / Models of neo-organism
