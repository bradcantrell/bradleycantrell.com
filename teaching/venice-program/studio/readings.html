Readings

Literature Review · Venice Lagoon Studio · Fall 2026

How to Use This Reading List

The readings are organized by the studio's four research lenses. Start with the five Key Papers — these are the foundational texts for understanding the lagoon's operating system. Then follow the lens most relevant to your research dossier. Each paper includes a studio-relevant note explaining how the findings inform design inquiry in the lagoon.

Key Papers — Start Here

Environmental Dynamics
Downstream changes in planform style and sedimentary processes in meandering rivers: Insights from the paleo Brenta River (Venetian Plain)
Ghinassi, M., et al.
Sedimentology, 2017
Establishes the morphological behavior of the paleo-Brenta system — the baseline for understanding every river diversion in lagoon history. Essential for Module 01: Reading the Territory.
Find on Google Scholar ↗
Environmental Dynamics
A linear relationship between wave power and erosion determines salt-marsh resilience to violent storms and sea-level rise
Tommasini, L., et al.
Journal of Geophysical Research: Earth Surface, 2019
The quantitative link between wave energy and salt marsh loss. Directly relevant to the lagoon's marsh edge dynamics and the design question of how to stabilize productive shorelines.
Find on Google Scholar ↗
Environmental Dynamics
MOSE effects on lagoon hydrodynamics and salinity
Mel, R.A., et al.
Ocean Engineering, 2022
Documents measurable shifts in salinity under MOSE operation. The key reference for understanding how infrastructure changes the productive conditions of the lagoon.
Find on Google Scholar ↗
Environmental Dynamics
Blue carbon stocks in Venice Lagoon salt marshes
Puppin, A., et al.
Science of the Total Environment, 2024
Quantifies carbon sequestration at 17,108 tons C/km2 — and the >30% reduction under MOSE. Carbon becomes a design metric for productive landscape interventions.
Find on Google Scholar ↗
Territorial Systems
Torcello archaeological survey: Urban form and transformation
Calaon, D. & Cianciosi, A.
Medieval Archaeology, 2020
The foundational archaeological study of Torcello's built environment. Essential for understanding the island as a designed territory — not just a site, but a precedent.
Find on Google Scholar ↗

By Research Lens

Productive Systems

Viticulture, aquaculture, horticulture — the material economies that shape the lagoon's form. 24 articles in corpus.

Productive Systems
Salt marsh vegetation and aquaculture: Valli da pesca as coupled productive systems
Cavraro, F., et al.
Estuarine, Coastal and Shelf Science, 2021
Artificial fish farm channels support similar ecological function to natural marsh habitat. Evidence that infrastructure can function as habitat — a core productive landscape concept.
Productive Systems
Dorona di Venezia: Recovery and cultivation of an indigenous lagoon varietal
Bisa, G. (practitioner knowledge)
Venissa Archive / unpublished
Primary practitioner source on the Dorona revival at Venissa. Root morphology, salt tolerance thresholds, cultivation calendar, microclimate design strategies.

Environmental Dynamics

Hydrology, sediment, salinity — the physical processes that define what can grow where. 22 geomorphology + 19 hydraulics + 14 ecology articles.

Environmental Dynamics
Tidal point bar morphodynamics: From a small cut to full morphological transformation
Bellizia, E., et al.
Earth Surface Processes and Landforms, 2021
The physical mechanism of scomenzar — how a small incision propagates into channel-scale morphological change. The quantitative basis for cut-based design in the lagoon.
Environmental Dynamics
MOSE gate closures under sea level rise: Projected frequency and duration
Michielotto, A., et al.
Regional Environmental Change, 2026
Projects +174 hours/month of MOSE closures under SLR scenarios. Quantifies the future salinity regime and its implications for productive landscape viability.

Territorial Systems

Settlement patterns, infrastructure networks, governance — the human organization of space. Torcello archaeology well-covered (16 articles).

Territorial Systems
Island connectivity and territorial governance in the Venice Lagoon
Grydehoj, A.
Island Studies Journal, 2019
Framework for understanding lagoon island connectivity as a design parameter. Who is connected to whom, by what infrastructure, with what consequences.
Territorial Systems
Cartographic traditions of the Venice Lagoon: 16th-19th century
Omodeo, P.
Nuncius, 2022
The lagoon's cartographic archive as a design resource. How the lagoon has been represented, measured, and understood through centuries of mapping.

Knowledge Systems

Traditional ecological knowledge, scientific monitoring, archival records, cartography — the ways of knowing that inform ways of making.

Knowledge Systems
eDNA monitoring and traditional ecological knowledge in lagoon fisheries
Various (ISPRA monitoring network)
Multiple sources
The intersection of scientific monitoring (eDNA, tidal gauges, satellite) with TEK from fishing communities. A gap in the corpus — primary fieldwork opportunity.
Knowledge Systems
The Venetian cadastral tradition: 1740-1812 as spatial knowledge infrastructure
Multiple sources; 1812 Napoleonic cadastre
Archivio di Stato di Venezia
The 1812 survey as a knowledge system — documenting vineyard distribution at peak extent. Now a baseline for restoration and design.
