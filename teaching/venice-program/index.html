Courses

ALAR 8010 / 4010 · Research Studio
Productive Landscapes of the Lagoon
Bradley Cantrell, William Sherman, Sandro Bisa
Tue + Thu · 12:30–5:00 PM
A research studio examining the northern Venice Lagoon as a productive landscape, exploring the design space between infrastructure and ecological process in a territory shaped by centuries of sediment choreography.
ALAR 5403 · Seminar
City and Landscape
Bradley Cantrell
Tue · 9:30 AM–12:00 PM
A field work course engaging the Venetian lagoon as a living laboratory for reading territory. Through direct observation, drawing, mapping, and structured field exercises, students develop practices of attention essential to working in complex landscapes.
ALAR 5203 · Seminar
Building Venice
William Sherman
Fri · 9:00–11:30 AM
An investigation of Venetian architecture as territorial practice, examining how the city's buildings, public spaces, and construction techniques emerged from and respond to the lagoon's particular environmental conditions.
ARH 5201 · Seminar
Art and Architecture of Venice
Monica Shenouda
Wed · 9:00–11:30 AM
A survey of Venetian art and architecture from its Byzantine origins through the modern era, read through the city's buildings, paintings, mosaics, and public spaces as primary texts.
