Italy Residency

September 19 – November 7. Based in Venice. No fixed course times; each week organized as a unified program with all faculty and students together. Weekends are free.

Week 5 · September 21–25
Mon Sep 21
Arrival + Settling
Arrive Venice Marco Polo. Settle into Venice apartments. Evening walk: canals, first observations of the city.
Tue Sep 22
Torcello: First Reading
Ferry to Torcello. Cathedral and Santa Fosca. Island perimeter walk. Field recording protocols.
Wed Sep 23
Venice Orientation
Venice as territorial system: canals, fondamente, campi. Architectural precedents walk with Sherman and Shenouda.
Thu Sep 24
Venissa + Viticulture
Vineyard tour with Sandro Bisa. Dorona as lens. Vineyard documentation exercise.
Fri Sep 25
Studio Desk Crits
First studio session in Italy. Field observations into proposition sketches.
