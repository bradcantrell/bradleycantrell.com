Project 05

Narrative Illustration

atmosphere · mood · composition
Final: Fri May 43ds max · Photoshop

Download the full Project 05 brief (PDF)

The semester has introduced several tools using 3ds Max — all of these tools have focused on creating elements to illustrate a single device, i.e. detail, vegetation, and terrain. In order to illustrate landscape it is important to understand that beyond objects, a successful image involves creating a convincing environment.

Using 3ds max and Photoshop you will create an image that captures a fictional landscape based on a passage from literature. This passage can be from any genre but should describe an environment, landscape, etc. In order to successfully create your image you will have to take into account atmosphere, mood, lighting, and composition.

Deliverables

  • 24 × 36 print of illustration, portrait or landscape
  • .tiff file @ 150dpi uploaded to the CADGIS server space

The final illustration is due Friday, May 4th @ 10:30am.
