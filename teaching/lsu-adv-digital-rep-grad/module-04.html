Project 04

Digital Terra

terrain modeling · Mississippi River
Due: Fri Mar 26 @ 8:30amCivil 3D

Download the full Project 04 brief (PDF)

In this phase of the project we will explore tools to represent landscape terrain models. You will represent a portion of the Mississippi River and surrounding landscape between Baton Rouge and New Orleans. The landscape and terrain of Louisiana is difficult to represent because there is very little topography. Using the methods we explore in class you will develop a strategy to represent and illustrate the topography — which can include the surface, stratum, planting, hydrology, etc.
