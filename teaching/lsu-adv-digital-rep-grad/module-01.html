Project 01

Modeling LSU Campus

three-dimensional modeling · digital site inventory · collaboration
Due: Wed Feb 7 @ 8:30amWeeks 2–4SketchUp · Google Earth

Download the full Project 01 brief (PDF)

This project will develop your skills in three dimensional modeling, digital site inventory, and collaborative use of digital media. Necessary tutorials and reference material will be posted to blackboard and lectures and lab sessions will give you hands on knowledge of the tools necessary to complete the assignment.

Assessment

Your grade for this assignment will be based on the best modeling practices document posted on blackboard. Your attention to detail and the craftsmanship of your models will be paramount in this assignment.

Deliverables

For this assignment you will place a copy of your final .kmz file, sketchup files, textures, and site photography on the class CADGIS serverspace. This information will be organized in the appropriate directories as per the example directory on the server.

The project will be due on Wednesday, February 7th @ 8:30am.

Schedule

  • Week 2 (Jan 22–26) — Class introduction: SketchUp / Google Earth; campus presentations; online lab.
  • Week 3 (Jan 29–Feb 2) — SketchUp and Photoshop.
  • Week 4 (Feb 5–9) — SketchUp; Project 01 due — review of campus model.
