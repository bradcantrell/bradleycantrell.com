Project 06

Animated Narrative

storyboard · 30-second animation
Storyboard: Fri Apr 13Exhibition: Fri May 43ds max · After Effects

Download the full Project 06 brief (PDF)

Using the same piece of literature you will create a 30 second animation that brings your imagery to life. It is important that your animation reveals a portion of your narrative that may not have been apparent in your illustration. The animation should bring life to your illustration rather than using a camera to “float” around elements. You will use 3ds max, Photoshop, and After Effects to composite your animation.

In order to understand your animation you will create a storyboard that focuses on your image swatch and timing.
