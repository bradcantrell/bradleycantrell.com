Project 03

Digital Vegetation

organic modeling · plant species
Due: Fri Mar 9 @ 8:30am3ds max

Download the full Project 03 brief (PDF)

The modeling tools you are using are adapted to create non-organic shapes. In order to expand your modeling repertoire you will construct a digital representation of a plant species.

You can choose any plant species that you have access to and that has enough content to create a successful model (a blade of bermuda grass isn’t enough content). You will need to photograph your plant species to document leaves, bark, and/or flowers. The images you capture will be used as reference material for your modeling and to create textures for all of the elements.

Deliverables

  • 11 × 17 print of illustration
  • .tiff file @ 200ppi uploaded to the CADGIS server space

The project will be due on Friday, March 9th @ 8:30am.
