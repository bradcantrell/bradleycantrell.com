Instrument Prototype & Precedents

prototype · precedents
Presentation: Mar 1Assignment 1

Download the full module brief (PDF)

In this module each pair of students will be familiarized with the basics of physical computing through a series of curated tutorials.

Each pair of students will also post resources they are finding to the Collab message board. One posting is required each week, accompanied by a few sentences that explains why this supplemental material is important to the development of the course and/or why it would be of interest to other students and/or the faculty in the course.

Curating Precedents

To develop the projects, each pair will also curate precedents to explain their instrument and interface. What is interesting about the projects you are finding (method, process, and/or implementation)?

Proof of Concept

Finally, using the tutorials and office hours sessions, each pair will create a proof of concept prototype. At this stage the prototype may not be sensing and/or visualizing the specific environmental phenomena intended, but instead serves as a model for the types of sensing and interactions that the pair would like to perform. This should also be supplemented by drawings, renderings, and/or other media that helps explain the project.

The working prototype and precedents will be documented in a Google Slides presentation.
