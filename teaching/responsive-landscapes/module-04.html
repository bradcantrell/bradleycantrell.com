In the final module, each pair will refine their instrument and interface to create a prototype and documentation. This will be supported by faculty office hours.

The Instrument

Students work individually or in small groups to create a physical computing device (instrument) that will sense and respond to environmental phenomena. This device will be created to either respond 1:1 with a physical landscape, a site that the student chooses, or be used in a live physical model such as the geomorphology table in 118 Campbell Hall or a physical model that the student or a group of students constructs.

The device will be a physical device that is a proof of concept, meaning that it should perform the basic functions but may also point to or conceptually demonstrate more advanced functions. Each group will also create an interface that will visualize and operate the device. Both the instrument and interface should be developed as design tools, extending the capabilities of the designer.

Documentation

The assignment also requires documentation of the process. This will require finding precedents and referencing code and the work of others, conceptually representing the need and purpose of the instrument/interface, creation of a tutorial to explain to others how they can also create the instrument and interface, and technical documentation such as drawings, schematics, and in situ drawings of the operation of the instrument and interface. This will be prepared in either a booklet or webpage, a template will be provided.
