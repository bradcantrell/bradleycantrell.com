4th Advanced Architecture Competition

Phase 01 engages students in the 4th Advanced Architecture Competition, an international competition on the topic CITY-SENSE: Shaping our environment with real-time data. The programme challenges participants to design one of two options — a self-sufficient building or a self-sufficient city — researching and demonstrating the impact that real-time data collection might have on sensor-driven habitats and cities. Participants are free to decide on the size and location of the project, which must be clearly detailed and justified, with any time projection (short, 10 years; medium, 50 years; or long-term within the 21st century) allowed.

Sites

The studio situates the competition work in a range of sites throughout Louisiana — the rural landscape of the Atchafalaya Basin and the urban setting of New Orleans. Site work focused on Morgan City, Butte LaRose, and the isolated settlements of the basin's hunting camps.

Site Research

  • Hunting Camps — history and case study
  • Urban / Rural Development — Butte LaRose and Morgan City
  • Cultural Context
  • Materials
  • Vehicular Circulation
  • Water Circulation / Navigation
  • Flood Control Infrastructure
  • Ecological Development and Evolution — vegetation, hydrology, animal life (marine and terrestrial)
  • Geology

Tech Research

  • Responsive Materials
  • Hardware
  • Software

Submission & Format

The proposals should be presented on three (3) DIN A-3 panels and comply with the following format:

  • Each panel presented in PDF format, each file no larger than 1MB.
  • The same panels presented in JPG format, 1024px by 768px, each file no larger than 500KB.

Video: a 1-minute (maximum) video explaining the key concepts of the proposal, with English subtitles (speaking is not mandatory; text, images, and animation may be used). Place your ID code at the bottom right of the video, upload to YouTube, and paste the link in your competition profile.

Written description: define a list of key concepts and exemplify your ideas by designing a specific building or city, or proposing changes for an existing one.

Graphic description: site plans, floor plans, elevations, sections, perspective drawings, photographs of physical or digital models, and axonometric drawings at any scale, with descriptive texts in English.

No physical materials will be accepted. All proposals must be in digital form and uploaded to www.advancedarchitecturecontest.org.
