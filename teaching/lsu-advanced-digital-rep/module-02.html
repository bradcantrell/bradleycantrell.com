Project 1.0 · digitalDIORAMA

Diorama

a landscape viewed through an animated camera
15 sec · 450 frames852 × 480 · 30fps3ds Max

Download the full Project 1.0 brief (PDF)

The diorama functions as a device to portray landscape as a spatial experience. The diorama provides an opportunity to create a landscape representation that is created from a limited vantage point. The vantage point and composition of the diorama is vital to creating a compelling experience. The goal will be to use one of the landscape typologies listed below to develop a digital diorama that will be viewed from an animated camera. It will be necessary to define the landscape typology with imagery collected from the internet or scanned from books. It is important to collect a diverse range of images and to carefully isolate and extract the elements necessary to create the diorama.

The diorama will explore the following concepts: background, middleground, foreground, atmosphere, scale, perspective, blending. The animation will focus on the first and last frame and the motion/timing between those points. It is not necessary to move the camera excessively but instead use the movement of the camera as a method to illustrate depth within the image.

Landscape Typologies

  • marsh
  • urban
  • agriculture
  • meadow
  • forest

Brief

For this project you will create a diorama in 3ds Max and render a 15 second animation. Your diorama will consist of “planes” that are textured with images. The diorama will be viewed through a camera that will be animated in a 15 second sequence (450 frames). The creation and setup of the planes and the extraction of elements from the images will be explained in detail during class.

The project will be assessed on creativity, clarity, attention to detail, and quality of craftsmanship.

Deliverables

  1. Typology swatches using provided Photoshop template
  2. 15 Second Animation — QuickTime file @ 30fps, 852 × 480 pixels, Sorenson, MPEG-4, or H.264 codec
  3. First Frame and Last Frame rendered as .JPG files rendered at 1920 × 1080 pixels

All deliverables will be submitted on the CADGIS server in the /students/project01/lastname_firstname folder.
