# Tennessee River Corridor — Your Reach

Twenty-one quads along the Tennessee River, each **2 km along the river × 4 km into the land**, aligned end-to-end to a single straight axis through downtown Knoxville. **Eighteen are assigned** (one per student); three are held in reserve.

Your quad is on the synthetic corridor map — **prompting-impact.pages.dev** — find your name in the left-hand roster.

## Reading your reach

Every quad reads the same river system at a different position. What changes is the reach, the adjacent land, and the question you ask of it.

- **River** — NWPS live stage + flow + forecast (gauge `knot1`, Tennessee River at Knoxville).
- **Weather** — Open-Meteo (temperature, humidity, wind, precipitation).
- **Air quality** — Open-Meteo (PM2.5, ozone, US AQI).
- **Species** — iNaturalist observations within your quad's radius.
- **Baseline** — NLCD land cover, USGS 3DEP elevation, FEMA flood zones.

## The assessment

You will use the `ecological-assessment` skill and `map-template.html` to build a live map of your quad and write the assessment. The assessment is only as honest as the data you can point to — every claim must land in the verification ledger.
