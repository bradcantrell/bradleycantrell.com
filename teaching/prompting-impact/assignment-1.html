❯ cat brief.mdthe premise

You have been assigned one reach of the Tennessee River, a 2 km × 4 km quad. Before the machine says anything about it, read it yourself.

❯ step 1 · open your quadgoogle earth

Your KMZ is in your folder in Session 1 (in the shared Drive).

1
Download your quad's KMZ file (named site-NN-your-name.kmz).
2
Open it in Google Earth. On desktop, File → Open, or double-click the file. On web or mobile, Projects → New project → Import KML/KMZ.
3
Double-click the quad to fly to it, then zoom until it fills the view.
4
See your coordinates
Click the quad once and a popup opens listing "Boundary corners (lat, lon)". Copy those four lines. You'll paste them into Claude in Step 3.
5
Save an image of the view. On desktop, File → Save → Save Image, or take a screenshot. On mobile, take a screenshot. You'll send it to Claude in Step 3.
❯ step 2 · read it yourselfyour eyes first
What you can SEE everyone answers
1
Water
flowing channel or slack reservoir/lake? how wide? natural (vegetated, meandering) or armored (concrete, riprap) banks?
2
Land cover
rough fraction of water / tree canopy / grass or field / pavement or buildings / bare ground?
3
Built form
roads, bridges, houses, industry, a dam, parks? dense or sparse?
4
Character
one phrase. Urban, suburban, rural, industrial, wild?
5
Surprise
one thing you didn't expect to find.
What you can DISCERN pick 2–3

These ecological phenomena are not readable from an aerial, but they leave traces and can be discerned or intuited. For each you pick, say what the static image lets you infer.

▸
Flow
slack impoundment vs a running channel, sediment plumes, exposed banks, a dam's pool?
▸
Life
canopy density, fields, wetlands, green-vs-brown. What's living here?
▸
Change
erosion scars, cleared land, construction, a shoreline that has moved?
▸
Air
visible sources of heat or pollution. Roads, industry, bare soil, parking?
▸
History
a dam, a straightened channel, old field patterns. How did it get here?
Your question

The one question. From what you've seen and discerned, what single ecological question do you want to ask of this reach?

❯ step 3 · ask claudetwo turns

Ask Claude twice, in this order. The order matters. Copy the prompts from prompt-template.md in your Session 1 folder, fill in your four corners (from your KMZ's description), and run them.

T1
Coordinates only, no image
Paste your four corners + the questions. Claude answers from coordinates alone, a guess based on what it thinks is there.
T2
The aerial
Attach the screenshot from Step 1 and ask "how would you update your answers?"

Claude outputs its answers in a fixed markdown format (see / think / discern / question), which you'll keep and submit.
