# Ecological Assessment / Audit — Template

Work through this top to bottom. Every factual claim must land in the verification ledger.

## 1. Reach + question

- **Reach:** (your quad on the Tennessee River corridor — number, coordinates, why you chose it)
- **Question:** (the one ecological question you are actually asking)

## 2. Baseline

What the reach is *now* — land cover, hydrology, built form, current conditions. Cite the sources (live data, land cover, your own observation).

## 3. Indicators

3–5 specific, measurable things to track, and why each matters:

| indicator | why it matters | source |
|---|---|---|
| | | |

## 4. Stakes

What changes — and for whom (species, people, infrastructure) — if the system shifts. Be specific, not "the environment."

## 5. Uncertainties

What the data does *not* tell you. Where is the machine guessing?

## 6. Verification ledger (required)

Classify every claim into one column:

| live-verified | machine-asserted |
|---|---|
| (source + timestamp) | (no source — suspect) |

A claim you cannot verify goes in the right column and is treated as suspect, not fact.

## 7. Critical reflection

What did the AI do well? Where did it mislead you? Where did your own judgment have to take over?

## 8. Disclosure

Tools used, data sources, and how outputs were produced / modified / rejected. Transparency is the design ethic.
