❯ cat brief.mdthe premise

Assignment 1 put one reach in front of two instruments. A model read it from four coordinate pairs, working from everything it had read and nothing it had seen. You then read the same reach from an aerial photograph with your own eyes. Where the two agreed, you learned something about the place. Where they parted, you learned something about the instruments.

That is the beginning of analysis rather than the end of it. A reach is never described by a single resource, and imagery is only the first. Historical photographs, a gauge standing in the water, more than eighty years of weather at your own coordinates, land cover, species records, and the bank under your own feet each hold something the others do not. None of them alone will answer the question you posed.

Assignment 2 assembles the panel. You are building a map of your reach that carries live data, historical data, and an accounting of where every number on it came from.

❯ step 0 · the operatorhow to work with the model

You will build this with a language model, and that is the intended way to build it. The model is the instrument you are learning to play. It is not the source of the answer, and you are the one who decides what counts. Every step below names what to hand it and what to ask for back.

Three rules hold across all six steps.

1
Paste the file
Describe nothing from memory. If the question is about your bounds, paste the KML. A model that cannot read your file will invent numbers that look exactly as plausible as the right ones.
2
Ask for the file, not the values
Coordinates retyped from a number on a screen are where this assignment goes wrong. Have the model write the file, then download it. You should never type a coordinate by hand.
3
Make it show its source
Ask which endpoint, which archive, which photograph. A layer with no source behind it cannot go in the ledger, and the ledger is the assignment.

If you are working in a chat window that cannot write files, ask for the contents in a code block and save the file yourself. The rule does not change. You never retype a coordinate.

❯ step 1 · the questionone sentence

Go back to the one question you wrote in Assignment 1. If the aerial changed it, use the new version. Write it at the top of your page, above everything else.

The map exists to answer that question, both explicitly and abstractly. This is the test of whether a layer of information belongs on it. A layer that does not help answer your question is ornament. At the same time it may not be clear initially what layers matter, so being able to build relationships is critical.

Say what would change your mind. Below your question, write one sentence naming the measurement that would prove you wrong. If nothing could prove you wrong, the question is not yet a question.
Feed the modelPaste your assignment 1 log, both turns, and the sentence you wrote. Ask it to test the sentence against the sources in step 2, and tell you which of them could prove it wrong.
❯ step 2 · gather the signalfour kinds of evidence

Gather as much as you can, then throw most of it away. Every source below was queried and returned data on November 9. They are grouped by what kind of evidence they are, and the kind is what your ledger is built from.

kindsourceswhat they give you
measured NWPS gauge knot1 Stage, discharge, forecast, flood categories. An instrument on the river near Knoxville, maintained by an agency that answers for it. Action 817 ft, minor 820 ft, moderate 825 ft, major 830 ft.
modeled Open-Meteo forecast, air quality, flood. NLCD land cover. FEMA flood zones. USGS 3DEP elevation. A documented method applied to data. Excellent at the scale it was built for, and carrying every assumption of that method.
observed iNaturalist Species recorded at or near your quad. A record is where somebody with a phone happened to walk.
archival Open-Meteo archive, 1940 to present. McClung Digital Collection. Planetary Computer scenes. Reanalysis weather back to 1940 for the grid cell nearest your quad, and a century of photographs of this river. A reanalysis is a model run over the past rather than a station record, and it returns the cell and not your point. Treat it as evidence rather than as a measurement.

Step 5 accepts two more kinds than the four above. Mine covers the layer you make yourself, and asserted covers a claim a model produced with nothing behind it. Four kinds to gather with, six to file under.

The live river gauge is not the one you expect. Five USGS Tennessee River sites sit inside Knox County and none of them serves live data. The river here is a regulated TVA reservoir reach, so USGS returns an empty array and the API looks broken. The live instrument is the NWPS gauge, not USGS. Start with knot1.
Required · the gap two numbers, one river

Two answers for the flow of the same river exist in this set. The NWPS gauge reports 55.3 kcfs, about 1,566 m³/s, measured at a dam-regulated reach. The Open-Meteo flood model, queried at the same coordinates, reports 266 m³/s.

Neither number is a lie. The flood model is a good instrument answering a different question. It does not represent how TVA operates these reservoirs, so it describes a river that is not managed the way this one is. Put both numbers on your dashboard, side by side, and write the paragraph that explains the difference. This is the clearest lesson in this corridor and you already hold both ends of it.

Feed the modelPaste the endpoint list from the data sources page and your bounds file. Ask it to fetch each source and write the response into data/, not to describe what the response would probably say. Anything it did not just fetch is asserted.
❯ step 3 · build the instrumentthree panels

Start from dashboard-starter.html. It already queries the gauge, the weather, the air quality, the flood model and iNaturalist, and it already computes the measured against modeled ratio. Open it, read it, and make it yours.

A dashboard has three jobs and they are not the same job.

1
Instrument panel
Live numbers that refresh. Stage, discharge, temperature, PM2.5, AQI. If nothing on your page changes on its own, you have made a poster.
2
Layer controls
Toggles for what you actually built. Species points, flood zones, land cover, elevation, historic photographs. Each layer answers a different question or it does not earn a toggle.
3
The ledger
Beside the live numbers, show the modeled ones and put a visible mark on every claim that came from a model without a source. The page should show its own uncertainty rather than hide it.
One layer has to be yours. It does not have to come from standing on the ground. Getting to your site in person is optional, and some of these reaches cannot be reached in the time you have. What is not optional is that one layer comes out of your own hand and your own reading rather than out of an endpoint. Draw a feature off the imagery in a drawing tool and export it as a layer. Count something across the whole quad and record the count and the method. Cut a section through the corridor from elevation data and draw what it shows you. Read a 1930s photograph closely and map the thing it holds that the current aerial does not. If you can get to the site, walk it and photograph what you find. Every API on this list can be called by anyone in ninety seconds, so a page assembled entirely from them says nothing about your reach that a page about any other reach would not also say. The layer you make is what turns the map into an argument about this 2 km.
Feed the modelPaste dashboard-starter.html and your one sentence. Ask for one layer at a time and ask which lines it touched. A model that rewrites the whole file leaves you unable to see what broke.
❯ step 4 · host itpublic url

Your map has to live at a real URL. A file on your laptop cannot be assembled with seventeen other maps and cannot be graded by anyone but you.

A
GitHub Pages
Make a public repo, put index.html and a data/ folder at the top level, then Settings → Pages → Source, deploy from branch, main, / root. Your map appears at yourname.github.io/repo-name within a minute or two.
B
Netlify Drop
Drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop, then sign in and claim the site. A drop you leave unclaimed stays behind a temporary password and is deleted, so a URL you never claimed will not work for me. Claim it and it stays up.

Test it in a private window while logged out. If it only works when you are signed in, it does not work.

Name it by site number, not by your name. Use a site-NN path and leave your name off the page title. The map is public and permanent, and there is no reason for a public page to carry your identity. Your site number is enough for me to know whose it is.
It has to be https on a real origin. Several of these endpoints refuse requests that come from a local file, which is a CORS rule rather than a bug you can fix. Prototype locally if you like, but the version you hand in must be served.
Feed the modelAsk for the exact clicks or commands for your host, one stage at a time, and paste back any error text exactly as it appeared. A paraphrased error sends the model after the wrong problem.
❯ step 5 · the handshakemeta.json

Every student hands in one small file in the same shape. That file is how your map becomes a piece of a single corridor map instead of eighteen unrelated pages. It is also your ledger in machine-readable form, so the honesty of it matters beyond this course.

{
  "site": 7,
  "name": "your name",
  "hosted_url": "https://yourname.github.io/site-07/",
  "center": [-83.9205, 35.9548],
  "bounds": [[-83.9381, 35.9452], [-83.9029, 35.9644]],
  "pulled": "2026-11-16T14:05:00-05:00",
  "one_question": "your sentence from step 1",
  "layers": [
    { "name": "live stage",  "kind": "measured", "source": "NWPS knot1" },
    { "name": "flood model", "kind": "modeled",  "source": "Open-Meteo flood" },
    { "name": "species",     "kind": "observed", "source": "iNaturalist" },
    { "name": "1920s photos", "kind": "archival", "source": "McClung" },
    { "name": "bank condition", "kind": "mine", "source": "traced from 2024 imagery" }
  ]
}

Do not type this file by hand. Every coordinate in it should arrive from a file the model can read, never from a number you copied off a screen. Paste the example above, paste your assignment 1 KML, and ask for meta.json. The only value you supply yourself is the URL you are hosting at.

Read the KML pasted below and write meta.json for my corridor map.

  rules
  take bounds from the KML exactly, do not round, do not reorder
  bounds is the lower left corner first, then the upper right corner
  every pair is [longitude, latitude]
  center is the midpoint of those bounds, same order
  copy my one_question verbatim, do not improve it
  one object per layer, each with name, kind and source
  kind is one of measured, modeled, observed, archival, mine, asserted
  if you cannot cite a source for a layer, use asserted

  mine to fill in
  site number    NN
  hosted url     https://...
  one question   my sentence from step 1
  my layers      listed below, with whatever I know about each source

  KML
  <paste your assignment 1 KML here>

Then check two things before you trust the result. bounds is the lower left corner first and the upper right corner second. center is longitude before latitude. Both catch people working from memory, and a model will only get them right if it is reading your file rather than guessing.

kind is what lets the corridor map color you honestly. Measured is a number from an instrument. Modeled is a number from a method. Observed is a person seeing a thing. Archival is evidence from the past. Mine is the layer you gathered yourself. If a value came from a model with nothing behind it, say asserted and do not dress it up. A map that admits one weak layer is more trustworthy than one that hides it.

❯ step 6 · the ledgersource quality

This is the part being assessed. Not whether your data is verified. Whether you can say what each source is good for, and which of your claims it can actually hold up.

Quality belongs to a pairing, a source and a claim about a specific 2 km reach. The same global flood model is excellent for screening a continent and useless for telling you what the water is doing at your quad. The judgment is always relative to the question.

Tiers of authority five, and you will touch all five
1
Instrumented by an agency that answers for it
The NWPS gauge. NWS warnings. A physical instrument, maintained by an organization that is accountable when it fails.
2
Modeled or derived, with a stated method
Open-Meteo, NLCD, FEMA flood zones. The assumptions of the method are usually the thing that matters.
3
Volunteer and crowdsourced
iNaturalist. Enormously valuable and unevenly distributed.
4
Archival and historical
McClung photographs, old maps, engineering records. A 1920 photograph is a real observation by a real observer, and it is one frame, chosen by someone, at one moment.
5
Machine assertion with no source
Anything a model told you that you could not trace. Useful as a hypothesis. It cannot support a claim about the world.
Six dimensions two sentences each

Authority. Who produced it, under what mandate, and do they answer for errors. Method. Measured, modeled, derived, observed, or recalled. Most disagreements between sources are method disagreements wearing a numbers costume. Resolution. Grain against your quad, and which axis fails. Latency. Realtime, hourly, daily, annual, or frozen in 2019. Fitness. Can it answer what you asked of this reach, or does it answer a neighboring question at a different scale. Stated error. Is an uncertainty given, or is the number bare. A source that publishes its error bars is easier to trust than one that does not, even when the error is large.

The weakest link rule. A claim inherits the quality of the weakest source in the chain that produced it. If you write that your reach sits at 811.6 ft, and that number came from a gauge four kilometers upstream in a different hydraulic reach, your claim is only as strong as that spatial transfer and not as strong as the gauge. The instrument is excellent. The transfer is not. Do this to three of your own sentences.
Feed the modelPaste your meta.json and ask it to argue for the weakest layer, not the strongest. What a source is good for is something it can find. What your claim needs is yours to decide.

❯ cat hard.mdthe trap

The temptation is to rank the sources once and reuse the ranking. It does not survive contact with the work.

The gauge is the best source in your set and it cannot tell you anything specific about your quad. iNaturalist is the weakest by method and it is the only source that knows a species is actually there. A 1920 photograph has no method at all and it is the only thing on your page that shows what this bank looked like before the dam.

Quality is a fit between a source and a question rather than a standing rank, and the whole skill is being honest about which fit you have.

Use whatever model you like to build this, the model or anything else. It does not go in the ledger. Only the source of the number does.
