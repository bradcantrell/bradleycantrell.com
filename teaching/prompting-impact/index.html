❯ cat briefing.mdthe premise

AI supporting environmentally responsive design by interpreting climate, material, landscape, and building-performance as interconnected systems. This module moves beyond generalized sustainability toward precise environmental consequence.

Eighteen students each take one 2 km × 4 km reach of the Tennessee River — a corridor of quads aligned end-to-end through downtown Knoxville. Each reads their reach as a living system with live environmental data, maps it, and audits what the machine actually knows versus what it merely asserts.

No generated imagery. The output: ecological systems mapsscenario studiesmaterial response diagramsshort design proposals

❯ ls lectures/three short lectures
01
The Instrument
Computation — now an LLM — is not an oracle but an instrument the designer directs. Knowledge of a living system is co-produced by human + instrument + landscape. Adaptive epistemology.
02
Reading the Living System
Environmental intelligence — how you read climate, hydrology, ecology, and material as interconnected dynamics, not isolated metrics. The assessment core.
03
The Neo-Wilds & Precise Consequence
The difference between greenwash and a defensible, specific ecological outcome — a system that behaves like neither nature nor city.

❯ cat assessment.mdthe deliverable

The deliverable is an ecological assessment of your reach of the Tennessee River, built around a live web map.

You read your quad with real-time data — river stage and flow, weather, air quality, species observations — then map it and argue what it means. The machine's role is explicit: it is a scaffold that must be audited.

live-verified
machine-asserted
NWPS river stage · Open-Meteo air quality · iNaturalist observations · NWS alerts
everything the LLM claims without a source — held to account before it is trusted
what the data actually shows
the reasoning the student must verify, correct, or reject
❯ cat skill.mdthe instrument, codified

A Claude skill ships with the module — a reusable recipe students hand to the model so it produces the live map and structures the assessment. It encodes: the workflow (reach → question → live data → map → assessment), the exact API endpoints, a Leaflet template, and the verification ledger that separates live-verified from machine-asserted.
