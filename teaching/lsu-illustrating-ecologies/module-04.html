Weight: 35%Culminating phase

The culmination of the semester will be an exhibition that represents the researched ecological system as a framework, process, and experience. Each group will determine, based on their research, the appropriate methods to represent each of these modes. This will be accomplished through prototype models, animations and/or illustrations.

Framework · Process · Experience

  • Framework — a basic structure underlying a system or concept.
  • Process — a series of actions or steps taken in order to achieve a particular end, or a natural or involuntary series of changes, or a systematic series of mechanized or chemical operations that are performed in order to produce or manufacture something.
  • Experience — (noun) practical contact with and observation of facts or events, or an event or occurrence that leaves an impression on someone. (verb) encounter, or undergo, or feel.

Each group will be responsible for designing and creating a stand alone exhibit that works with each of the other groups in the class as a single exhibition. The students and professor will decide on a series of common attributes that will be similar across the group work.

Research Groups

Algae Cultivation — Joaquin Martinez · Andrew Collins · Lauren Fasic. An economy of trade, efficiency of production, and an alternate energy source — large-scale algae production to generate biofuel, supported by political incentives and social acceptance.

Bayou Bienvenue Wetlands — Lance Fulton · Robert Seemann. Two former wetland zones at the eastern edge of New Orleans, once a freshwater cypress/tupelo swamp, devastated by saltwater intrusion following the MR-GO; explore restoration possibilities through research and animation.

Agricultural Runoff — Patrick McGannon · Kristen Lonon. Nitrogen and phosphorus pollution as a leading cause of water degradation; modeling existing watershed conditions and methods of filtration and runoff treatment.

Urban Golf Course Water Recycling — Zhujun Wang. Investigate water recycling models in urban golf courses, capturing water through the course and its surroundings to minimize water use.

Fluctuating Infrastructure — Drew Baum · Alex Ramirez. The Mississippi River’s flow, shaped by levees, dams, spur dikes and locks; what happens to sediment and debris as the river cuts and fills the surrounding land.

Mississippi River Delta Formations — Katherine Tabor · Jessica Carvajal. The delta built by alluvial deposits at the mouth of the Gulf of Mexico; isolating sediment and demonstrating its origin, flow, and depositional patterns.

Gulf Coast Gas and Oil Infrastructure — Andrea Galinski. Louisiana’s oil and gas infrastructure; three processes — dredging canals (incision), salt water intrusion (infiltration), and fragmentation of marshland vegetation (disintegration).
