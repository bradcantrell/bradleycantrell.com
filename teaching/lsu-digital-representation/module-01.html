Landscape representation is primarily about envisioning environments. This requires a broad understanding of how each component of our world contributes to a larger view of the spaces we occupy. The class will self-organize in groups of 3–5 students and develop a theme for their work throughout the semester. Your theme should be based on tangible sources you document from film, music, literature and/or interactive media. Your group should think about how you would combine these sources — it is not enough to pick a video game you like and use that aesthetic to guide you through the semester. What are the key features that touch you, and what do they contribute to an environmental narrative?

Pick a group of individuals that you will work with for the semester; your group should have a common thematic interest. You will present your group’s theme in the form of a PowerPoint presentation as well as a developing asset library. Your PowerPoint presentation should show the sources you are using for your theme and how you envision them creating landscape, objects, and architecture.

Your presentation will be assessed on creativity, clarity, attention to detail, and quality of craftsmanship.

Deliverables

  1. PDF or PowerPoint presentation
  2. Group name, ideally based on your theme
  3. Theme assets — images and/or video library

All deliverables will be submitted on the CADGIS server in /students/groups/groupname.
