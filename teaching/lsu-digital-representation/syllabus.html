Overview

This course presents contemporary digital representation methods specific to landscape architecture. There will be exploration of methodologies, techniques, and theories occurring in digital representation in contemporary practice. Projects will be an application of this research. The pedagogy will focus on strategies to marry the analog visualization and drawing techniques explored in LA 1101 with contemporary software and digital media. The goal is to tool students with digital techniques for both measured and diagrammatic drawings that facilitate a more mature design process.

Learning Objectives

  1. Bridge the fundamental gap between the analog tools learned in LA 1101 and contemporary digital tools used to present landscape architecture projects.
  2. Understand the concepts, tools, and techniques associated with AutoCAD.
  3. Execute measured drawings based on known data, field measurements, and aerial photography.
  4. Transfer digital drawings between Autodesk and Adobe software.
  5. Understand and utilize Illustrator tools for diagramming and conceptual purposes.
  6. Apply knowledge of digital drafting techniques to develop plans and sections specific to landscape architecture.
  7. Understand raster concepts and design tools associated with Adobe Photoshop.
  8. Generate illustrative site plans and supporting documents.
  9. Apply file linking between Adobe products.
  10. Annotate and diagram presentation boards using Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign.

Software & Tools

  • AutoCAD — measured drawing, line weights, and paper-space layouts.
  • Adobe Illustrator — vector diagramming and conceptual work.
  • Adobe Photoshop — raster concepts, textures, and illustrative renderings.
  • Adobe InDesign — annotation, layout, and presentation boards.

Course Text

Digital Drawing for Landscape Architecture: Contemporary Techniques and Tools for Digital Representation in Site Design, Bradley Cantrell and Wes Michaels.
