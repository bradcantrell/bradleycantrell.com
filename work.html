Work

2025-present
Adaptive Environments Lab
Research Lab

A UVA research initiative designing places that promote human and ecological health through sensing networks, incremental adaptation, and computational partnership.
2025–present
Sediment Bridge / Sediment Interface
Design Interface / Research Instrument

A browser-based sediment model that lets designers play fluvial processes into form and validate each exploration against precise physics, released publicly as the Sediment Interface, with a research build, the Sediment Bridge, that closes the loop live against a CFD solver.
2017-present
UVA Geomorphology Lab and Adaptive Design Protocols
Research Infrastructure / Pedagogical Platform

A multi-purpose platform supporting PhD student investigations, speculative advanced studios, and foundational instruction through physical simulation of fluvial and coastal processes.
2021
Indeterminate Futures
Digital Exhibition, NFT Archive

Translated years of geomorphology table documentation into a distributed digital archive, minting incremental snapshots of sediment flow experiments as NFTs throughout the 2021 Venice Architecture Biennale.
2019
Algorithmic Cultivation
Installation / Manifest

An installation exploring the interrelationship of human, machine, and plant through a robotic pruning system that shaped living vegetation according to data streams external to the growing environment.
2019
Failure, Storm Signals
Video Installation

A video essay for the Storm Signals exhibition series cataloguing environmental failures across two centuries, presenting failure as a constituent element of environmental history.
2018
Failure, Drawing Codes
Drawing

A mixed-media drawing for the Drawing Codes book and exhibition, exploring how landscape representation might engage indeterminacy, error, and the limits of prediction.
2017
Coastal Paradox
Speculative Design / Competition Entry

Finalist in LA+ Imagination Ideas Competition, Islands
2014-2017
Responsive Environments and Artifacts Lab (REAL)
Research Infrastructure / Manifest

Co-directed with Allen Sayegh at the Harvard GSD, REAL investigated responsive environments at the intersection of embedded computation, material assembly, and environmental sensing.
2016
Branding Islands Making Nations
Speculative Exhibition / Film Installation

An exhibition developed with the Vertical Geopolitics Lab examining the relationship between physical land creation and the branding of territorial identity on constructed islands.
2016
Responsive Topography
Exhibition

An exhibition at the Harvard GSD Experiments Wall presenting the Responsive Environments and Artifacts Lab's research on choreographing hydrological energy to construct land through real-time sensing, robotics, and adaptive management.
2013-2014
DredgeFest
Public Program / Workshop / Field Investigation / Symposium

A series of public programs organized by the Dredge Research Collaborative treating dredging as a cultural, political, and ecological practice, with Cantrell leading the Adaptive Devices workshop.
2011-2014
Mississippi River Sediment Diversion Visualizations
Trans-disciplinary Research / Visualization

A trans-disciplinary academic studio effort commissioned by the National Wildlife Federation to explain coastal wetland loss and potential solutions for rebuilding the Louisiana coast.
2013
Fort Proctor
Practice / Design Research

A design research project engaging a Civil War-era fortification standing entirely surrounded by water in Lake Borgne, Louisiana, as a site for speculating on adaptive preservation in conditions of radical landscape change.
2010-2013
Coastal Sustainability Studio / TiKI Lab
Practice / Trans-disciplinary Research Laboratory

Trans-disciplinary research centers at LSU dedicated to coastal resilience, community adaptation, and territorial design at the intersection of ecology, engineering, planning, and landscape architecture.
2012
Sedimachine
Manifest / Prototype

A prototype apparatus investigating the relationship between controlled water delivery, sediment deposition, and surface morphology, exploring how small modifications to flow conditions might choreograph sediment accretion.
2009
Over/Under
Speculative Design / Competition Entry

Proposed a vertical relationship between a surface-level garden and the underground subway station beneath it, using a single citrus tree as a living datum connecting the two realms.
2006-2007
Surface Tension
Speculative / Hybrid Model

Explored the boundary between physical landscape models and digital interactivity, producing cast landscape surfaces embedded with electronics, LEDs, and sensors that responded to environmental input.
2006
Thresholds Installation
Speculative Installation

An interactive installation that translated spatial occupation into topographic representation through a high-contrast mural and camera-based blob detection, reimagining the contour line as a dynamic notation.
